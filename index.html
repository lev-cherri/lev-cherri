The Club

Built on spirit, ambition and community.

Chelmsford RFC is more than a rugby club. It is a place where players, supporters, and local families come together around dedication, respect, and the joy of the game.

Whether you are taking your first steps in junior rugby or looking to play at senior level, Chelmsford offers a welcoming, competitive environment that values effort, team culture, and long-term development.
